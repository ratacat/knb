## Sedaye Mardom - IUST email disciplinary defenses

URL: https://www.sedayemardom.net/?p=161523

Published: 2026-04-30T07:48:49Z. Sedaye Mardom quotes the Iran University of Science and Technology student-rights working group: students had received SMS messages requiring them to send disciplinary defenses by email by 12 Ordibehesht. The group says this conflicts with the 1403 disciplinary procedure because students should first be informed of charges in person and then attend an in-person defense session; it also cites Article 82's note that remote or telephone meetings and decisions based on them are invalid.

## Sedaye Mardom / Zamaneh - Sharif letter rejects tiered internet

URL: https://www.sedayemardom.net/?p=161385

Published: 2026-04-26T12:28:44Z. Sedaye Mardom, citing Radio Zamaneh, reports that Sharif University's student council and scientific-association assembly wrote to the science minister rejecting Internet Pro / tiered internet. The letter says internet access is an indivisible public right, not a privilege for selected groups; four months of restrictions had paralyzed many educational and research activities, stopped projects, blocked international faculty/university communications and article submissions, and forced students/researchers toward expensive VPNs or Internet Pro. It says the policy increases inequality, forces unsafe workarounds, creates "legal rent," and risks extinguishing young people's hope.
