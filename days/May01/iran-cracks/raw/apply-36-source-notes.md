# v31 source notes - campus control and restricted reopening

## Scope

Followed the open campus question after the April 29 dorm/internet pass: whether virtual classes, dorm closures, internet restrictions, and named student arrests are becoming a coordinated post-war student-control model or only isolated reprisals.

## Exa and direct-page checks

- Exa search for university virtual classes and war policy surfaced SNN's April 29 article quoting the science minister. Direct page metadata and article text say the war is a turning point for higher education and requires reconsidering science and technology policy, educational programs, the university calendar, and student admission capacity.
- Exa search for dorm closures and war damage surfaced Tasnim's April 21 report on Shahid Beheshti University. Direct page text says six student dormitory units, mainly housing about 2,000 women students, were out of service after recent attacks; sports halls, educational/research buildings, one research institute, and laboratories were seriously damaged; the university estimated total damage at about 6,500 billion tomans and dorm reconstruction at 300-400 billion tomans.
- Exa search for Alzahra dorm return surfaced a Tasnim April 25 notice. Direct page text says students could enter dorms only from 6 to 8 Ordibehesht, 08:00-13:00, to collect personal belongings. It explicitly says accommodation was not possible under any circumstances and students had to leave immediately after collecting belongings.
- Several Vista/tag search hits around disciplinary cases and tense reopenings were old February 2026 pages despite fresh index dates. They were excluded from current May claims.
- Exa did not surface clean current Persian primary pages for Ali Alirezai or Fariborz Kohnzad beyond existing KNB/xPool-style reports.
