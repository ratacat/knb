Hossein was arrested… https://t.co/3lnMTwFH6z\n\n# Sasan Azadvar, Tenth Protester Hanged Within 42 Days\n\nIran Human Rights (IHRNGO); 30 April 2026: State media have reported the execution of Sasan Azadvar, a young protester arrested in connection to the January protests in Isfahan. He was accused of throwing stones, using a club to break the windows of a police minibus, attempting to procure petrol to set the vehicle on fire during and inciting others to join the protests. IHRNGO has established the location of his execution as Isfahan Central Prison.\n\nThis morning, IHRNGO published a report identifying 44 protesters at risk of execution, a figure that included Sasan. With his sentence carried out today, at least 43 individuals remain at risk of execution, including two women and three teenagers who were reportedly under the age of 18 at the time of their arrest.\n\nIHRNGO Director, Mahmood Amiry-Moghaddam stated: “While the world’s attention remains focused on the war and ceasefire negotiations, the authorities continue to execute protesters. The Islamic Republic views its own people, especially the youth, as its primary existential threat, and uses the death penalty as a tool to silence dissent and maintain power.”\n\nAccording to the judiciary’s Mizan News Agency, Sasan Azadvar, a 21-year-old Kyokushin karate champion and protester arrested in relation to the January protests in Isfahan, was hanged at an unspecified location on 30 April 2026. IHRNGO has established the location of his execution as Isfahan (Dastgerd) Central Prison. He was sentenced to death on the charge of moharebeh (enmity against God) through “throwing stones, using a club to destroy police vehicle windows, attempting to set the minibus on fire, and inciting the public to riot,” which the Revolutionary Court explicitly ruled was an act of \"effective cooperation with the enemy\" carried out at “the official invitation of the Zionist regime and the US president.”\n\nSasan was arrested on 3 January, per informed IHRNGO sources. According to the state report, his forced confessions were admitted as formal evidence to corroborate the state's charges. No independent information is available regarding the circumstances surrounding his case. IHRNGO previously reported that Sasan’s family were summoned to the prison for the final family visit on 28 April.\n\nSasan Azadvar is the tenth protester executed in relation to the December 2025/January 2026 protests. The implementation of death sentences in Iran had been largely halted following the start of US and Israeli military attacks on 28 February.\n\nThe execution of protesters arrested in relation to December 2025–January 2026 began 42 days ago, on 19 March. Authorities have announced the execution of nine protesters thus far. Saleh Mohammadi, Saeed Davodi and Mehdi Ghasemi were hanged in Qom Central Prison on 19 March. Amirhossein Hatami, was hanged on 2 April.
