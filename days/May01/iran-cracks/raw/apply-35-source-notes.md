# v30 source notes - Bazaar follow-up and market freeze

## Scope

Followed the open May 2-4 Bazaar/Ferdowsi/Aladdin questions after v20/v21: whether the 27-29 April Bazaar protest/partial closure grew into a renewed Saturday strike, or whether the regime contained it through quiet, price controls, stock-market closure, and guild/security pressure.

## Exa

- Searches for Aladdin/mobile-market/Ferdowsi/Tehran Bazaar strike or closure after 1 May did not surface a clean source-near report of a renewed physical strike.
- Indexed Persian media instead showed market-management stress:
  - Stock market trading remained stopped at least until Monday 14 Ordibehesht, and reopening was described as dependent on conditions/requirements and information transparency.
  - Aftab/Vista carried a mobile-market report saying mobile prices had risen 30-40% in recent months, with stopped import registration and customs-cost changes pushing consumers toward repair.
  - Fararu framed the FX market around doubt/uncertainty: domestic dollar and gold rose even as global gold fell.
- Search hits around the dollar showed continued 180k/185k chatter, but most indexed items were generic price-table pages rather than direct market reporting from Ferdowsi.

## Read

The Bazaar crack did not disappear; it changed form. Visible collective merchant action was not verified in this pass, but price discovery and liquidity remain abnormal: the exchange is still formally stopped, reopening is conditional, mobile prices are up sharply, and FX chatter remains unstable. Treat this as contained collective action plus unresolved market stress, not market normalization.
