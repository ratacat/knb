# v32 source notes - medicine budget and insurance rationing

## Scope

Refreshed the health-access lane without duplicating v16/v17 rows already in KNB. Existing active claims already cover late-April drug price jumps, insulin access stress, rare-disease insurance warnings, worker/retiree treatment deferral, and a prior xPool medicine sample.

This pass tested whether the medicine crack has moved since then into clearer official budget/insurance rationing or organized patient mobilization.

## Exa and direct-page checks

- KhabarOnline/Mahr, 24 April, says about 5,000 drug items were repriced around the end of 1404 and start of 1405, but basic insurer coverage remains unclear. It quotes Food and Drug Organization head Mehdi Pirsalehi saying new prices were sent to the Supreme Insurance Council on 7 Farvardin to run from 15 Farvardin, but resource uncertainty meant the changes were not fully applied in systems, creating problems for people.
- Mehr, 23 April, says Social Security exempts hemophilia, thalassemia, and dialysis patients from copays only for drugs inside base-insurer commitment lists, and that the special/hard-to-treat patient fund pays part of costs. This is official containment language, but narrow.
- NourNews, 27 April, quotes the Food and Drug Organization head saying 1405 preferential FX for food/drugs is $3 billion, with $1.5 billion for medicine and pharmaceutical raw materials, while projected medicine/raw-material import need is $2.2 billion. It says from $1.1 billion net preferential FX, the drug office can cover at most half the need; the other half must use non-preferential FX. It also says 65% of medicine/equipment debt was paid by the end of 1404 and about $400 million of 1405 resources would go to past customs/clearance settlement.
- ILNA, 20 April, remains the strongest direct patient-side diabetes source already in KNB; it was rechecked to avoid duplicating it. It reports multi-city insulin access failures, dose rationing, insurance-coverage reduction, test-strip/sensor price increases, and medium/long-term complication warnings.
- Exa did not surface a cleaner new primary patient association protest after 1 May beyond existing rare-disease/cystinosis and foundation-insurance items.

## Read

The health crack is no longer only anecdotal pharmacy pain; official budget and insurance mechanics now describe rationing. Preferential FX covers only part of expected medicine/raw-material need, some 1405 FX is already earmarked for old debt/clearance, and thousands of repriced medicines still depend on opaque and incomplete insurer/system updates. The regime has narrow relief language for hemophilia, thalassemia, and dialysis, but rare-disease groups and patient-side insulin reporting show that the gaps are broader. As of this pass, visible patient mobilization remains weak; the stronger signal is quiet treatment triage plus budget/insurance rationing.
