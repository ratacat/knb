Published: 2026-04-21T07:19:28Z / 10:49 1405/02/01. SNN reports Deputy Science Minister and Student Affairs Organization head Saeed Habiba visited Shahid Beheshti damaged dormitories. Six student dormitory units, mostly girls' dorms housing about 2,000 students, lost habitability and need complete rebuilding. Sports halls and some educational/research buildings were severely damaged; one research institute and internal labs were 100% damaged. Habiba said virtual education continues but practical/lab work requires rapid rebuilding.

## ILNA - minister says internet cut hurts university resilience

URL: https://www.ilna.ir/%D8%A8%D8%AE%D8%B4-%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%DB%8C-5/1779321-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%DB%8C%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%AA-%D8%A8%DB%8C%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%84%D9%84-%D8%A8%D9%87-%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%A8-%D8%A2%D9%88%D8%B1%DB%8C-%D8%A7%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%DB%8C-%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B4%DA%AF%D8%A7%D9%87%DB%8C%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%84%D8%B7%D9%85%D9%87-%D9%85%DB%8C-%D8%B2%D9%86%D8%AF

Published: 2026-04-28T14:48:55Z. ILNA reports Science Minister Hossein Simai told a joint parliamentary education commission session that 231 scientific centers were targeted, international communication disruption was a major challenge, and cutting international internet damages the social resilience of academics. He also described support packages for science-park companies with less than 20% layoffs, special support for students whose homes were destroyed, and flexibility such as allowing graduate defenses without the article requirement.

## Iran Online - Sharif students still lack international internet

URL: https://inn.ir/news/article/150789/%D9%85%D8%B4%DA%A9%D9%84-%D8%AF%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%B3%DB%8C-%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%AC%D9%88%DB%8C%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%87-%D8%A7%DB%8C%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%AA-%D8%A8%DB%8C%D9%86%E2%80%8C%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%84%D9%84-%D8%AD%D9%84-%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%AF%D9%87

Published: 2026-04-23T12:26:14Z. Iran Online quotes Alireza Zarei, Sharif University's ICT center director, saying faculty international-internet access had been mostly provided through ministry servers and faculty mobile lines, but students still faced serious problems accessing international internet, external scientific resources, and international communications. Students could access domestic university services only; the university had no independent authority to provide special internet packages; exam mode had not yet been decided.

## IranWire - IUST email disciplinary defenses despite internet cut

URL: https://iranwire.com/fa/news-1/151821-%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%DA%A9%D9%85%D9%87-%D8%A7%DB%8C%D9%85%DB%8C%D9%84%DB%8C-%D8%A8%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%BA%D9%85-%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%DB%8C%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%AA-%D9%81%D8%B4%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B2%D9%87-%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%B4%D8%AC%D9%88%DB%8C%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%85-%D9%88-%D8%B5%D9%86%D8%B9%D8%AA/

Published: 2026-04-30T07:01:18Z / 10 Ordibehesht 1405. IranWire, citing the Iran University of Science and Technology student-rights working group, reports that several IUST students received SMS messages requiring them to send disciplinary defenses by email by 12 Ordibehesht. The students were connected to Dey and Bahman 1404 protests. The group said this happened while universities were remote and internet had been cut for more than two months, and that the process contradicted 1403 disciplinary rules requiring in-person charge notification and defense.

## Melliun / IranWire / United Students - Birjand disciplinary sentences

URL: https://melliun.org/iran/523972
