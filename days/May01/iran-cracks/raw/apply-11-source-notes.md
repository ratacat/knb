## Iran Online / Seda va Sima - industrial electricity until 20 Ordibehesht

URL: https://inn.ir/news/article/151310/%D8%AA%D8%A3%D9%85%DB%8C%D9%86-%DA%A9%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D9%82-%D8%B5%D9%86%D8%A7%DB%8C%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D8%A7-%DB%B2%DB%B0-%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AF%DB%8C%D8%A8%D9%87%D8%B4%D8%AA

Published: 2026-04-29T10:18:01Z. Iran Online reported remarks by Mostafa Rajabi Mashhadi, Deputy Minister of Energy for electricity and energy. He said industrial electricity-management restrictions started on 15 Farvardin last year, but this year they would be delayed and industries could fully use electricity until 20 Ordibehesht. He also said decisions after that would be weekly, based on temperature, consumption, and production conditions, and that industries whose dedicated power plants were damaged would be supported and asked to repair/rebuild.

## Jamaran via Vista - production units cut over "transit" electricity fees

URL: https://vista.ir/n/jamaran/o1jie

Published: 2026-04-25T02:48:50Z. Vista carried Jamaran's article. It says several production-unit managers complained that provincial electricity companies had cut power even when electricity consumption charges were paid, because "transit" charges were unpaid. One producer said conditions are wartime, production is effectively absent, transit costs were imposed, and requests for time were answered with weekly payment or lump-sum/installment pressure.

## ILNA - Fars industries and Dena tire workers

Published: 2026-04-22T09:38:55Z. ILNA interviewed Bahram Zanoobi Tabar, head of the Fars Province Supreme Labor Council. He said some units are operating stop-start because of raw-material shortages; war conditions and damage to mother industries such as petrochemicals and steel disrupted the supply chain, including imported materials from Malaysia and Indonesia through sea routes. On Dena tire, he said the factory has about 1,147 workers; a proposal to use unemployment insurance for about 1,100 workers was rejected; workers were told to return; unemployment insurance is only temporary medicine; layoffs have become a national challenge.

## ILNA - Siaden Abhar steel workers

URL: https://www.ilna.ir/%D8%A8%D8%AE%D8%B4-%DA%A9%D8%A7%D8%B1%DA%AF%D8%B1%DB%8C-9/1776553-%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%AA%D8%BA%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%AD%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%AF-%DA%A9%D8%A7%D8%B1%DA%AF%D8%B1-%D8%AF%D8%B1-%D9%81%D9%88%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D8%B3%DB%8C%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%85%DB%8C%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B1%DB%8C-%DA%A9%D8%A7%D8%B1%DA%AF%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A8%D9%87-%D8%A8%D9%87%D8%A8%D9%88%D8%AF-%D9%88%D8%B6%D8%B9%DB%8C%D8%AA

Published: 2026-04-23T08:00:00Z. ILNA quoted workers at Siaden Abhar steel saying about 250 contract workers are employed. Wages are still delayed: Esfand 1404 and Farvardin 1405 are owed, and about 15 reinstated workers are still owed Bahman wages. Workers say last year's electricity-cut problem caused several temporary shutdowns, production restarted with help, and production accelerated from Farvardin; their priorities now are current wages and preserving jobs through increased production.
