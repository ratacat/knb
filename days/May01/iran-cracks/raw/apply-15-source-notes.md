## Payamema - Semnan Bar statement
- URL: https://payamema.ir/payam/150677
- Published: 2026-04-26T12:32:52Z / 6 Ordibehesht 1405
- Signal: Reproduces Semnan Bar Association's statement rejecting differentiated internet access.
- Core detail: The statement says access to information is a fundamental right, not a privilege; differentiated access conflicts with justice, equality, and human dignity; the bar refuses such access and asks policy-makers to base internet access on inclusive justice, transparency, and equality.

## Zoomit - Isfahan Bar rejection
- URL: https://www.zoomit.ir/iran-news/459229-isfahan-bar-rejects-prointernet-access/
- Published: 2026-04-27T09:48:37Z
- Signal: Reports that Isfahan Bar Association's board met on 6 Ordibehesht and unanimously rejected Internet Pro.
- Core detail: The report quotes the bar's reasoning that limited free access without a fair mechanism for other citizens conflicts with equality, justice, Article 20, and the legal profession's public-rights mission.
- Limitation: Official Isfahan Bar page was not directly recovered through Exa; Zoomit explicitly attributes the wording to the bar website.

## Zoomit - Graphic Designers Society
- URL: https://www.zoomit.ir/iran-news/459208-graphic-designers-union-about-internet-pro/
- Published: 2026-04-26T17:30:57Z
- Signal: Reports the Iranian Graphic Designers Society's notice.

## Payamema - Tehran E-Commerce Association statement
- URL: https://payamema.ir/payam/150823
