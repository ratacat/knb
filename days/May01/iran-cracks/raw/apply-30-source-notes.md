## Interpretation

Teacher Day follow-up currently looks managed rather than independently coordinated. The regime and its aligned networks are successfully flooding the visible public lane with loyalty, martyrdom, and teacher-honor messaging. The crack is that the honor frame has to coexist with unresolved retiree ranking/pension complaints, salary and inflation pressure, school war-damage management, and continuing coercion against teacher activists. For now this resolves the v23 follow-up question toward "managed channel still dominant", with a watchpoint for independent teacher-council statements or repeat actions after the ceremony window closes.
