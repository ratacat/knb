- Teacher Day channel:

## Interpretation

May Day did not produce a verified nationwide labor strike in this pass. It did expose a tri-layer pattern: official channels absorb demands into loyal ceremony, source-near labor reporting keeps surfacing concrete survival claims, and social discourse remains politically charged but fragmented.
